Myrmidon Bridge options

The extension connects outbound to the bridge gateway only. Pairing, connect and disconnect live in the toolbar popup.

What this build implements:

  • read-only actions: open, read, click, screenshot
  • allowlist of tender-site domains, checked locally before every action
  • the bridge token stays in chrome.storage.local on this computer

Signing steps are never automated: the person at this computer presses the button on the final step; the extension does not hold the key or the PIN.
