Myrmidon Bridge options

The extension connects outbound to the bridge gateway only. Pairing, connect and disconnect live in the toolbar popup.

What this build implements:

  • actions: open, read, click, fill, download, screenshot
  • a confirmation step: an action the board marks for confirmation runs only after you press Confirm
  • allowlist of company domains, checked locally before every action
  • the bridge token stays in chrome.storage.local on this computer

Signing steps are never automated here: the extension does not hold the key or the PIN, and it declares no sign capability at all.
